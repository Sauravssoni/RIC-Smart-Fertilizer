# Security and claim discipline

RAJ-URVARA is an evaluator sandbox. No Government credential, Aadhaar value, farmer PII or production secret belongs in this repository.

- `LIVE_PUBLIC`: published, non-sensitive public evidence.
- `CONTRACT_READY`: integration contract only; credentials/authorization are required.
- `DEMO_SANDBOX`: synthetic data or local simulation.

The SLM is not an agronomic decision authority. Numeric fertilizer recommendations must originate from authorized deterministic rule/services. Anomaly scores are human-review prioritization only.
