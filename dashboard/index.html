POLICY CONTEXT

Balanced-use opportunity for Rajasthan

PM-PRANAM links state incentives to reduction in chemical fertilizer use relative to a three-year average. A 2025 parliamentary answer said Rajasthan had not achieved such reduction for FY 2023-24. RAJ-URVARA therefore treats balanced-use intelligence as an operational target — not as a guaranteed grant claim.

Incentive formula50%of fertilizer subsidy savings, subject to scheme rules and actual qualification

DATA & SECURITY

Federated by default

  • PII stays in authorized systems; minimal references cross services.
  • Raw sensitive evidence stays off the ledger.
  • Purpose, consent and access are auditable.
  • India/state hosting and security controls remain deployment requirements.
  • Every model/action is versioned with provenance.
HUMAN AUTHORITY

AI proposes. Government decides.

  • No LLM-generated agronomic dose.
  • No autonomous subsidy denial or enforcement.
  • No automatic district transfer without approval.
  • No quality certification from anomaly models.
  • Overrides remain first-class, reason-coded records.
