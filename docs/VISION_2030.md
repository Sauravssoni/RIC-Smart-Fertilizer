# Vision 2030 — Rajasthan Agricultural Input Intelligence Rail

RAJ-URVARA begins with the Smart Fertilizer challenge because fertilizer has the clearest combination of agronomic need, subsidy exposure, stock-planning complexity and multi-party physical movement. The long-term asset is broader: a reusable, Government-governed intelligence rail for agricultural inputs.

## 2026 — Smart Fertilizer pilot

- FarmGraph evidence twin
- DemandCast probabilistic planning
- governed NutrientTwin / UrvaraRX
- fairness-constrained FlowOpt
- StockPulse + tiered GPS/IoT
- TraceGraph + Quality/Leakage Sentinel
- SUTRA assisted offline execution

## 2027 — State fertilizer operating layer

Scale validated modules to more districts, grades, warehouses and dealer networks. Add department-approved integrations, season-to-season model monitoring and centralized State mission control.

## 2028 — Agricultural input trust rail

Reuse the same primitives for micronutrients, bio-inputs, selected seed/input logistics, quality evidence and authorized advisory workflows. Keep authoritative registries with their owners; reuse consent, lineage, decision receipts and custody events.

## 2029 — Predictive agri-logistics

Combine crop/soil/weather intelligence with warehouse capacity, transport lead-time, route risk and seasonal demand to reduce emergency transfers and improve last-mile service levels.

## 2030 — Rajasthan Agricultural Digital Twin

A statewide evidence and decision layer where approved systems can answer: what is needed, where, when, why, what is available, what moved, who authorized it, and what outcome followed.

The objective is not a monolithic database or an autonomous AI authority. The objective is a **federated, explainable and auditable State intelligence layer** that makes Rajasthan's existing agricultural digital infrastructure more predictive and operationally useful.

## Commercial principle

Syntheon monetizes implementation, integration, model operations, field deployment, support and reusable platform modules — never farmer personal data. Statewide economics are to be scoped only after pilot evidence establishes data readiness, hardware tiering and operating complexity.
